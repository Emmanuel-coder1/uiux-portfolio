UI/UX DESIGN · MOBILE APP

JobHub

A mobile recruitment experience designed to make discovering opportunities and connecting with potential candidates simpler and more intuitive.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Designing a simpler recruitment experience.

The Challenge

Recruitment platforms can become overwhelming when users have to navigate through too much information before finding relevant opportunities or candidates.

The Goal

The goal was to create a clean, straightforward mobile experience that allows users to quickly discover opportunities, review profiles and communicate with the right people.

MY APPROACH

From structure to meaningful interactions.

01

Research

Understanding the needs of job seekers and recruiters helped define the core experience.

02

Structure

I organized the key information and interactions to create a simple navigation flow.

03

Interface

The interface was designed around clarity, accessibility and visual consistency.

04

Refinement

The layouts and interactions were refined to create a smoother overall experience.
