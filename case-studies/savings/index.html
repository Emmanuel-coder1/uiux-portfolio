UI/UX DESIGN · FINTECH · MOBILE APP

Savings App

A personal finance mobile experience designed to help users manage their money, track savings goals and stay organized with their financial activities.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Making personal finance easier to understand.

The Challenge

Managing savings and personal finances can become confusing when important information is scattered across different screens and actions.

The Goal

The goal was to create a clear financial experience that allows users to understand their balance, manage savings and quickly access important financial actions.

DESIGN APPROACH

Designing around clarity and financial confidence.

01

Discover

Identify the key information users need when checking their savings and financial progress.

02

Organize

Structure financial information into clear sections that are easy to understand and navigate.

03

Design

Create a modern interface with clear visual hierarchy and accessible interactions.

04

Refine

Refine the interface to maintain consistency across the different financial experiences.

THE DESIGN

A complete savings experience.

KEY EXPERIENCE

Designed around the user's financial journey.

Financial Overview

A clear dashboard helps users quickly understand their financial position and access important actions.

Savings Goals

Dedicated savings goals help users visualize their progress and stay focused on what they are working toward.

Quick Actions

Frequently used financial actions are made easier to find and access.

Contributions

Contribution functionality gives users a structured way to manage their financial commitments.

TOOLS & SKILLS

Built with intention.

Figma UI Design UX Design Mobile UI Prototyping Fintech Design

FINAL THOUGHT

Financial products should feel simple, clear and trustworthy.
